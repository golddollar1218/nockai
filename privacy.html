Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

This site does not collect personal information. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Third parties

The page loads fonts and icons from Google Fonts and unpkg, which can see your IP address when your browser requests them. Links to Hugging Face and other sites are governed by their own policies.

Models and your text

NockAI models are designed to run on your own device. Text you translate locally is not sent to us.

The "Ask Nock Coder" box on the home page answers your own questions live. When you type one, the question is sent through our server to Surplus (surplusllm.com), which runs the model and returns the answer. We do not store questions or answers, but Surplus processes them under its own terms. Do not enter personal data, keys or other secrets. The suggested example prompts are answered in your browser and are not sent anywhere.
